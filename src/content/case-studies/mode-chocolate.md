## The challenge

Mode needed to build brand awareness in the keto and healthy-snack category and earn retail shelf space without a large paid media budget.

## What we did

We built a micro- and nano-creator program across Canada and the US, running outreach, gifting and content collection. In parallel, retail and stockist outreach used organic creator content as evidence of consumer demand.

## The outcome

Mode established authority in its niche, generated millions of organic views and expanded from DTC-only distribution into a stable network of retail and stockist partners.

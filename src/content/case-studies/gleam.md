## The challenge

Gleam wanted durable authority around the commercial keywords buyers use when evaluating giveaway and contest platforms.

## What we did

We ran a consistent backlink program targeting authoritative marketing, SaaS and tool-review publications, with priority on pages tied to commercial intent.

## The outcome

Gleam reached a domain rating of 83, built 1.2 million organic backlinks and appears prominently in search and AI recommendations for high-intent giveaway queries.

## The challenge

Perplexity's paid growth team needed to test thousands of creative ideas every month with fresh creator faces, multiple styles and multi-country coverage.

## What we did

We embedded inside the growth team's workflow and built an always-ready in-house creator roster, managing briefs, production and delivery at high volume.

## The outcome

The creator operation helped support simultaneous launches across markets and gave the growth team the creative throughput required to test at exceptional pace.

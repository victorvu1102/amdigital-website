## About the client

Founded in 2022 in San Francisco, Perplexity is one of the fastest-growing AI technology start-ups in the world. Backed by Nvidia, SoftBank, Jeff Bezos, and NEA, the company has raised over $1.2 billion and reached a $20 billion valuation by late 2025, with approximately 30 million users and $500 million in projected 2026 revenue.

## The challenge

Perplexity needed to scale their creator operation fast — testing thousands of new creative ideas every month across multiple countries simultaneously. They needed an in-house roster of quality creators ready to go at any time, with fresh faces, multiple styles, and multi-country coverage. The workload reached thousands of videos per month.

## What we did

We embedded directly inside Perplexity's paid growth team, operating within their workflow and building their in-house creator roster from the ground up. We ensured a continuous supply of fresh creator faces across multiple styles and countries, managing briefs, production, and delivery at the volume the growth team required — reaching thousands of videos per month at peak.

## Our success

Our work contributed to Perplexity becoming the number one downloaded app in the US, India, and Brazil, and supported launch campaigns across multiple countries simultaneously. We built a stable, always-ready creator roster that gave the growth team the creative firepower to test at a pace and scale most teams cannot sustain.

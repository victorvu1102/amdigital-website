AI discovery rewards brands that are easy to understand, verify and connect to a category. That starts long before a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for a recommendation.

## Build a clear entity

Use one consistent company name, description, expertise statement and contact footprint across your owned profiles. Search and answer engines need to know that every reference points to the same organization.

## Publish evidence, not claims

Detailed case studies, named methodologies, expert authorship and measurable outcomes make a brand more useful as a source. Generic service copy does not.

## Structure every answer

Use descriptive headings, direct definitions, lists where they add clarity and concise summaries. This helps people scan your content and makes the information easier for machines to retrieve accurately.

## Keep the foundation technical

Fast pages, canonical URLs, crawlable HTML and valid structured data remain essential. GEO is an extension of strong web fundamentals—not a replacement for them.

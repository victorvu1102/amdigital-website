Vietnam rewards brands that localize the whole go-to-market motion, not only the campaign copy. The channel mix, creator brief, offer, landing page and response cadence all shape whether a global proposition feels relevant locally.

## Start with audience behavior

Map where discovery, validation and purchase actually happen for your category. Facebook, TikTok, Zalo, search and marketplaces can play very different roles depending on the product and audience.

## Localize the operating system

Build local review into creative production, media buying and community management. A fast feedback loop between market insight and execution matters more than a perfect translation delivered late.

## Measure the market honestly

Define a small set of commercial signals before launch. Use the first campaigns to learn which message, channel and offer combination deserves more investment.

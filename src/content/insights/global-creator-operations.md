The visible part of a creator campaign is the content. The part that determines whether it scales is the operating system underneath it.

## One brief, local nuance

Global consistency should live in the campaign objective, claims and quality bar. Local teams still need room to adapt creator selection, language and cultural context.

## Throughput needs stages

Separate sourcing, vetting, briefing, production, review, payment and reporting. When each stage has a clear owner and service level, bottlenecks become visible before deadlines slip.

## Automation supports judgment

Automation is strongest at repetitive tasks: building candidate lists, checking required fields, scheduling follow-ups and consolidating reports. Human judgment remains essential for fit, tone and quality.
